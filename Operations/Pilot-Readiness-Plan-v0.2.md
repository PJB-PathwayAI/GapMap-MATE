# Pilot Readiness Plan v0.2
## Status

Version: 0.2 (Engineering Review)

## Mission

Validate the MATE Experience Blueprint with real service leavers whilst preserving every behavioural principle established by the doctrine.

The objective of the pilot is not feature completeness.

The objective is learning.

### Current Phase

Pilot Readiness

The philosophy, doctrine, behavioural design and engineering foundation are considered complete for MVP.

The project now transitions from design into implementation.

## Pilot Objective

Demonstrate that MATE improves the quality of transition decisions through evidence-based companionship.

The pilot exists to validate:

- Behaviour
- Trust
- Understanding
- Partnership
- Decision Readiness

—not product features.

## Pilot Cohort

### Initial Pilot

- 5–10 Service Leavers
- Representative of the target audience
- Recruited through trusted partners where possible
- Selection based on transition profile rather than technical confidence

Potential recruitment:

- BFRS
- SSAFA
- Existing trusted network

## Pilot Environment

Prior to pilot commencement the following must be confirmed:

- Conversation interface
- User authentication
- Deployment environment
- Data storage
- Export capability

The environment should prioritise reliability over scalability.

## MVP Build Slice

The MVP shall expose only those capabilities required to validate the Experience Blueprint.

### Included

- Onboarding
- Understanding
- Capability Discovery
- Personal Operational Picture (POP)
- Decision Readiness
- Transition Partnership
- Conversation Memory
- Export

### Excluded

- Guardian Protocol automation
- Enterprise integrations
- Azure migration
- Advanced analytics
- Organisation features
- Future enhancements

## Safeguarding

Guardian Protocol remains outside MVP.

During pilot:

- Human oversight remains available.
- Manual safeguarding escalation is defined.
- Participants may pause or withdraw at any time.

Safety takes precedence over pilot objectives.

## Data & Consent

All pilot participants shall:

- Provide informed consent.
- Understand how information will be used.
- Retain the right to withdraw.
- Retain the right to request deletion of their information.

## Success Criteria

The pilot will be considered successful if participants:

- Feel understood.
- Understand themselves more clearly.
- Can explain their decisions.
- Trust Smudge.
- Demonstrate increased confidence and independence.

These outcomes align directly with the Behavioural Hierarchy established within the Experience Blueprint.

Success is measured through transformation.

Not engagement.

## Pilot Duration

Recommended duration:

4–6 Weeks

Pilot completion triggers:

- After Action Review (AAR)
- Behaviour Review
- Engineering Review
- Recommendation for wider deployment

## Feedback Loop

The pilot forms part of the established PROOF methodology.

Pilot

↓

Exercise

↓

After Action Review

↓

Behaviour Review

↓

Engineering Review

↓

Doctrine Review (only if required)

↓

Next Iteration

Doctrine changes should remain exceptional.

Engineering refinement is expected.

## Build Principles

Every implementation shall preserve:

- Psychological Safety
- Trust
- Shared Understanding
- Partnership
- Transformation
- Behavioural Judgement
- Independence

The Experience Blueprint remains the constitutional authority throughout implementation.

## Commander Question

Before approving the pilot ask:

"Would I be comfortable putting Bodge through this tomorrow?"

If the answer is no...

The pilot is not ready.

## Exit Criteria

The pilot concludes when:

- Success criteria have been reviewed.
- Participant feedback has been analysed.
- Engineering observations have been documented.
- An After Action Review has been completed.
- A recommendation has been made for the next phase.
