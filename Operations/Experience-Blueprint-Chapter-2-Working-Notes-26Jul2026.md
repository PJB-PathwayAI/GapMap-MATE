## Chapter Scope

Chapter 2 answers the broader question:

> "How does Smudge earn the right to become a trusted companion?"

The emotional journey is one dimension of this, not the entire chapter.

---

## Key Concepts Defined in This Session

### First Conversation

The first conversation is about building trust, not onboarding.

### Behavioural Doctrine Zero

The conversation exists to build understanding. The populated fields are a consequence of the conversation, not its purpose.

### The Reflection Moment

"Can I tell you what I'm hearing?" — the mechanism by which Smudge mirrors shared understanding back to the user. Not a data dump. A check-in that says "I've been listening."

### Personal Operational Picture (POP)

The POP is the visualisation of shared understanding — not a dashboard reveal.

- POP is generated by shared understanding, not profile completion.
- A dashboard says "here's what we know about you." The POP says "here's what we've built together."
- The POP marks the transition from conversation to a shared operational picture of the user's journey.

### Psychological Safety During Discovery

Psychological safety is a consideration during discovery, not a barrier to discovery. (Now locked as standalone doctrine — see `Doctrine/Psychological-Safety-During-Discovery-v1.0.md`.)

### Emotional Outcomes

The emotional outcomes the experience is deliberately designing for:

- **Relief** — the feeling of being understood
- **Joy** — the feeling of recognised capability
- **Morale** — the feeling of forward momentum

## Behavioural Hierarchy (Emerging — Not Yet Doctrine)

Cipher identified what is becoming the conceptual spine of the experience:

1. **Psychological Safety** — enables trust
2. **Trust** — enables understanding
3. **Understanding** — enables the Personal Operational Picture
4. **Personal Operational Picture (POP)** — enables Decision Readiness
5. **Decision Readiness** — enables Independence
6. **Independence** — "I've got this."

Each layer is a dependency for the one above it. If the foundation layer is absent, the layers above cannot be genuine.

**Status:** Not yet formalised as doctrine. Will likely be referenced across the Blueprint.

---

## Engineering Implications (Emerging)

No architectural changes. Behavioural and doctrinal refinement only.

The engineering implications are becoming clearer:

- POP is generated by shared understanding, not profile completion
- Psychological safety is a consideration during discovery, not a barrier
- Relationship preservation takes priority over information gathering
- The POP marks the transition from conversation to shared operational picture

Implementation is deliberately deferred until the experience is fully defined.

---

## Design Sequence

The agreed sequence guiding all work:

```
Mission → Doctrine → Experience → Architecture → Engineering
```

Each layer constrains the next. Engineering never drives. Experience never operates without doctrine. Doctrine never exists without mission.

This sequence has prevented designing around the technology instead of the service leaver.
